Manitas suggests simple crafts made from cardboard tubes, paper plates, egg cartons, bottles, clothes pegs and whatever is usually in the house. Tell it what is in your drawer and it shows you what you can make, step by step and in 3D, with templates to print at real size.

Nine crafts are free and complete. All 60 unlock with a single payment: no subscription, no ads, shared with Family Sharing.

Frequently asked questions

What ages is it for?

Each craft says from what age a child can do it almost alone (3 to 10) and what a grown-up should do or watch.

How do I print the templates?

In the craft, tap Print: it comes out at real size on A4 or Letter. The test line should measure 5 cm.

I changed devices and cannot see my purchase

Go to Settings › Restore purchase with the same Apple Account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Everything is inside the app. You only need a connection to buy or restore.
